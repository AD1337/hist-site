# Podcast Co-host and Community Manager.
We are looking for a person to be both a podcast co-host in a new history podcast and a community manager that will engage with our community on Discord and social media.

## Details
- **Location**: Remote.
- **Type**: Part-time, freelance contract.
- **Hours:** 2–5 hours per week (possibly longer hours later on).
